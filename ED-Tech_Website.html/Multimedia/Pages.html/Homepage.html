About US

At Coding Ninjas, we believe anyone can learn to code, but the right guidance turns a coder into a world-class engineer. Founded in 2016 by alumni from IIT and Stanford, we set out with a clear mission: to revolutionize tech education. We bridge the massive gap between traditional college curricula and the rapidly evolving demands of the global software industry.

Today, we are one of India's leading ed-tech platforms, empowering students and working professionals to upskill, master cutting-edge technologies, and land their dream jobs at top product-based companies.
